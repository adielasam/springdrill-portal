Assessment Setup — all terms, all levels
ComponentMax ScoreSub-Term

Subject total = 1st CAT + 2nd CAT + Exam, out of 100. Identical for Ember, Lent and Trinity terms and for every year group (verified on Educare). Half Term reports use the two CATs only; the Exam column appears at Full Term.

Result Workflow
Draft → Submitted → Approved → Published

Teachers save drafts, then Submit Final (irreversible — the batch locks). Admin approves or rejects on Approve Reports. Admin publishes per class/term on Publish Report — only then do students see their cards. Rejected batches return to the teacher for correction.

Averages and positions are computed live from stored scores whenever a view loads, so there is no separate Process run that can go stale.

Collation Rules
  • Average = sum of subject totals ÷ number of subjects taken. Subjects with no entered score are excluded.
  • Position = rank by average, descending. Tied averages share the same position and the next position is skipped (12th, 12th, 14th).
  • 2-Term Average = mean of the First and Second Term averages. 3-Term Average = mean of all three term averages. Terms with no scores are left out.
  • Cumulative names are plain words — Educare's P A / C P / P B F codes carry no meaning on screen and are not used here.
