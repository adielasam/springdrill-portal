Set up your school

Complete these five steps once. You can change everything later from Settings.

Enter the one-time setup code the SpringDrill team sent you. It works once — after that it stops working.

Lost your code, or it expired? Codes last 14 days — ask the SpringDrill team for a fresh one.

1. School details

Paste the web address of your logo image. File upload comes later.

2. Classes

Add the classes your school runs, e.g. Year 7, Year 8 … Year 12 (or JSS / SSS names).

3. Subjects

Add the subjects your school teaches.

4. Fees

Add your school's fee items. You can add more later from the Fees page.

5. Staff

Create teacher accounts, and a second school admin if you want one. Students are created the same way as today, from the admin pages.
